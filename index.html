Osciloom

Osciloom is a next-generation, cross-platform Digital Audio Workstation (DAW) and 8-Engine Polymorphic Synthesizer Suite designed for music production and sound design built to deliver a fluid user interface.


Summary


Osciloom is a cross-platform digital audio workstation (DAW) and synthesizer suite developed by Jamazing.
It is designed as an all-in-one environment for music production and sound design, combining sequencing, synthesis,
mixing, modulation, automation, and mastering tools in a single application.

It currently targets Windows and Android and is listed as v1.0.0 Beta 4.




What does Osciloom offer?


Osciloom combines a DAW with eight different synthesis engines:


  • FM synthesis – 6-operator FM with 32 algorithms
  • Virtual Analog – including PWM, sync and sub-oscillators
  • Wavetable – 3D wavetable manipulation and morphing
  • Granular – 2D granular synthesis
  • Sampler – a 16-pad groovebox-style sampler
  • Physical Modelling – including Karplus-Strong synthesis
  • DrumSynth – analogue-style percussion synthesis

    • ...and much more!
